https://github.com/LeoninCS/GCFeed at commit
`8cf995cfa03985c335594c453ea050df5c0f1b6a`.

`github.com/ZoeySigel/owlet-video/backend`. Owlet compatibility extensions and
migration changes are tracked in `docs/gcfeed-migration.md`; these additions
